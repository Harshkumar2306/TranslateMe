## 3. Observations
1. **Asymmetric 8E/4D Latency Advantage**: Decoding into English averages **0.26s–0.29s** per sentence on Apple Silicon `mps`, while Indic targets average **1.40s–1.99s** due to longer subword segmentation.
2. **Direct Indic↔Indic Generalization**: Direct non-English directions (`hi→ta` 45.02 chrF++, `te→hi` 40.07 chrF++, `te→ta` 40.86 chrF++) translate without English pivoting at inference time.
3. **Zero Script Contamination or Repetition Loops**: Target script masking and 3-gram blocking yield **0.0%** empty outputs and **0.0%** consecutive word repetition across all 48 held-out tests.
